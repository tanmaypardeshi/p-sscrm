P-SSCRM is a framework for managing software supply chain security risk, organized into Groups, Practices, and Controls. Each Control maps to established external standards — including NIST SP 800-161, SLSA, and BSIMM — to help organizations align their supply chain security practices with recognized industry benchmarks.

This site documents the framework and its mappings, and will support community contributions as it matures.
